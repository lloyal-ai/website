# Hero material

`liquid-field.webp` is the approved original image-generated monochrome optical
material from the homepage design studies. It was encoded as WebP at quality 88.
Its material references are mercury, plasma and silk; there are no model marks,
interface elements or third-party artwork in the image.

The source remains a quiet full-field still. The ambient renderer applies slow
coordinate deformation beneath a stationary frosted layer. The same asset is
the fallback for reduced motion, unavailable WebGL and context loss.
