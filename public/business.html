Lloyal for businesses and organisations

AI that you control, not AI that controls you.

Lloyal puts AI inside your own software, on machines you own. You decide what it can reach, and you can check every step it takes while it works.

01 / The problem

Most AI asks you to take it on trust.

01

Your data leaves the building.

Client files and internal documents go to someone else's servers, and you can't see what happens to them there.

02

The model can change overnight.

A tool your team relied on last week starts answering differently this week, and nobody asked you.

03

You find out afterwards.

When the AI does something it shouldn't, you're piecing it together after the damage is done.

04

Staff don't wait.

If there's no approved option, people paste work into whatever chat tool is open in the next tab.

Being wary of AI is reasonable. What helps is AI you can actually check.

02 / What you get

Three things change when the AI is yours.

More control.

The model only changes when you change it. A provider can't swap it out or quietly make it worse.

Completely private.

Your data stays on your devices and your network. Nothing leaves unless you choose to send it.

Nothing to set up by hand.

Your staff open it like any other app. There's no model to pick and no keys to paste.

03 / How it works

Set the rules, and check the work while it runs.

With most AI, the model sits on a provider's server. Your software sends it requests over the internet, and you review what happened afterwards.

Lloyal puts the model inside your application. Your rules run in the same place the AI does, so they apply at every step as it happens.

Control

It only reaches what you allow.

You grant the AI exactly the documents and tools it may use. Anything else is refused before it runs.

Custody

The model is yours to keep.

The model is a file on your hardware. You choose it and you decide when it changes.

Audit

Every step goes in your log.

Each step the AI takes is recorded in your own log as it happens. Read along live, or go back through it later.

06 / For your technical team

The detail your IT team will ask for.

RUNTIME
The model runs in the same process as your application, through HDK (Harness Development Kit). No API key and no separate inference server.
MODELS
Open-weight models in GGUF format, from small models on a laptop to frontier-scale models on your own infrastructure. The model is a file path; changing it doesn't change your application.
PERMISSIONS
Your application grants tools to the AI. A protected tool without a grant is refused before it executes, and the refusal is recorded as an event.
AUDIT
Every agent step is emitted as an event (agent:spawn, agent:tool_call, agent:tool_result, agent:return, tool:error). Your application writes those events to your own log.
NETWORK
Inference is local. Data leaves only through tools you grant, such as web search, which sends the search query.
SET-UP
npx lloyal-ai new scaffolds a TypeScript application. On first run it downloads the model and verifies its digest.
